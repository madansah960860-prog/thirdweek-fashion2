Canyon Chore Jacket

Register VS-0431 · 1990s · US L · one unit only

$148

A three-pocket chore jacket in heavy cotton duck. The grey-tan across the shoulders and cuffs is twenty-odd years of real wear, not a factory wash, and it sits lighter at the seams where the cloth moved most. The body is cut straight with no waist shaping, so it hangs square and keeps its line with the pockets loaded. This is the jacket that gets worn five days a week from March to May, then again through September.

It came out of an estate clearance in Clackamas County in July 2026, alongside two pairs of work trousers that could not be saved. This one needed a wash, one button and nothing else.

How it was prepared

Washed, mended, measured, plated

The jacket arrived in July 2026 and was machine washed cold, inside out, then line dried. One steel tack button had gone missing at the placket; a matched vintage tack replaced it in August 2026, which leaves four originals and one honest substitute. Nothing else was touched. The hem chain stitch was checked under a lens and left alone, the paint mark on the left hip was not removed, and the fade at the cuffs was not evened out.

It was then measured flat on the table with a steel rule: chest 23 in, shoulder 20 in, sleeve 25.5 in, length 28 in. The plates were shot in August 2026 across four views, the collar label and the replaced button among them, so both are on the record before you buy.

Cloth and make

  • Shell 100% cotton duck, approximately 12 oz per square yard
  • Lining Unlined, with flat-felled seams throughout the body
  • Pockets Two lower patch pockets, one chest patch pocket, all fully bagged
  • Buttons Five steel tack buttons, four original, one replaced with a matched vintage tack in August 2026
  • Stitching Original chain stitch at the hem, intact, no drop stitches found under a lens
  • Label Woven collar label present and legible, photographed in view four

Measured flat

  • Chest, flat 23 in across the front, one inch below the armhole, so 46 in around
  • Shoulder 20 in seam to seam
  • Sleeve 25.5 in from the centre back of the collar to the cuff
  • Length 28 in from the base of the collar to the hem
  • Cut Boxy and straight, no waist suppression, hem sits below the hip
  • Layering A mid-weight crewneck fits underneath with room. A heavy Aran does not

Care

  • Wash Machine wash cold on a gentle cycle, turned inside out
  • Dry Line dry. A hot tumble dry will take about half an inch off the body length
  • Press Steam press on medium if you want the collar to sit sharp
  • Never Do not bleach. Duck cotton of this age yellows unevenly and permanently
  • Expect It will keep fading. That is the point of the fabric

Size and condition

  • Assigned size US L, based on our own measurements, not the original label
  • Condition grade Good vintage
  • Flaws Fade at both cuff edges, a paint mark roughly the size of a dime on the left hip, one replaced button
  • No Holes, moth damage, odour, or repairs to the shell
  • Fit reference Our fit tester is 5 ft 11 in and normally wears a US medium. On them this is deliberately loose through the body with the cuff at the wrist bone
  • Second reference On a 6 ft 1 in wearer who takes a US large, it fits as a standard chore coat with a shirt underneath
