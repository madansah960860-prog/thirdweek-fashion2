Sending it back

Returns and exchanges

Thirty days from delivery, on everything including made-to-order pieces. No restocking fee, and no justification required for a change of mind.

30 days from delivery No restocking fee Exchanges shipped both ways on us $8 label fee on a change of mind Refund inspected within 3 business days

Who pays what

We pay: exchanges

Swap for any other piece in the register and we cover shipping in both directions.

We pay: our mistakes

Anything described wrongly, damaged in transit or sent in error. Shipping both ways is on us.

You pay: change of mind

An $8 prepaid label fee is deducted from the refund. Nothing else is withheld.

The sequence

How a return runs

  • Email within 30 days of delivery

    Send your order number, the register number of the piece and one line on the reason. For a change of mind, "it does not suit me" is a complete answer and we will not ask for more.

  • We send a prepaid label within one business day

    The label arrives by email as a PDF. Print it, or ask for a QR code if you would rather have the carrier print it at a drop-off point.

  • Pack it as it arrived

    Reuse the original box if it is intact. Put the record card back in with the garment. The card is how we identify the piece when it lands back on the table.

  • Drop it off within 14 days of the label

    Labels expire after 14 days. If yours lapses, tell us and we will issue another at no charge.

  • We inspect and refund within 3 business days

    Inspection is the same lamp check every piece gets at intake. Provided nothing has changed, the refund goes back to the original payment method the same day.

Refunds reach most cards within three to five business days of us processing them, though the exact timing is set by your bank rather than by us.

The swap

Exchanges

We cannot exchange a garment for the same garment in another size, because there is no other size. What we can do is exchange it for any other piece currently in the register.

  • Tell us the register number you want instead and we will hold it for you for 48 hours while the return is in transit.
  • If the new piece costs more, we send a payment link for the difference. If it costs less, we refund the difference.
  • We pay shipping in both directions on an exchange. There is no label fee.
  • The 30-day window on the new piece starts from the day it is delivered, not from the original order.

Store credit

If you would rather have credit than a refund, we add ten percent to the value as a thank you, and the credit never expires. This is entirely optional and we will not push it. A refund is always available.

What comes back

Condition on return

A returned piece needs to come back in the condition it left in. That means unworn beyond trying it on, unwashed, unaltered, and with the record card included.

We are not looking for reasons to refuse a return. Try the garment on properly, walk around in it, sit down in it, see how it feels over the layers you would actually wear. That is not wear. That is how you find out whether something fits.

What we cannot accept back is a piece that has been laundered, altered by a tailor, or worn out of the house. All three are irreversible on secondhand clothing and all three change what the garment is.

The short list of exceptions

  • Gift cards are not returnable or refundable for cash, as stated at the point of purchase.
  • Pieces altered at your request. If we shorten a belt or a hem for you before shipping, that change cannot be undone and the piece is final sale. We always confirm this in writing first.
  • Anything laundered or altered after delivery, for the reasons above.
  • Returns started after 30 days. Write to us anyway. We have made exceptions for genuine circumstances and we would rather hear from you than not.

Nothing on this page limits your rights under applicable US consumer protection law, including any rights against a faulty or misdescribed item.

Our error

If the record was wrong

Every flaw is meant to be photographed and named in the condition line. Find one that is not and that is our error, and we treat it as our error.

Send a photograph and we will pay shipping both ways, refund in full, and correct our record. We also go back through the intake notes to work out how the flaw was missed, because a mis-description usually means a step in the inspection was rushed rather than that the flaw was invisible.

This has happened four times since the business started. Each time we published the correction rather than quietly fixing the listing.
