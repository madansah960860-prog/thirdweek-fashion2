What a cookie is

A cookie is a small text file a website asks your browser to store, and to send back on subsequent visits. Some are essential, in the sense that a shopping cart cannot remember its own contents without one. Others exist to measure traffic or to target advertising, and those are entirely optional.

The same rules on this page apply to similar technologies including local storage, session storage and pixels. Where we say cookie, read it as covering all of them.

Your choice, and when we ask

On your first visit a banner appears at the bottom of the screen with three options: accept all, reject non-essential, or manage preferences by category. The banner does not block the page, does not appear as a full-screen interstitial, and does not reappear once you have chosen.

No analytics or advertising cookie is set before you choose. If you close the browser without choosing, nothing optional is set and you will be asked again next time. If you reject, we record only that you rejected and nothing else changes.

Your choice is stored for twelve months, after which we ask again. You can change it at any time using the cookie settings link in the footer of every page.

Google specifically

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalised advertising by Google at Google Ads Settings, and from many other vendors at optout.aboutads.info or optout.networkadvertising.org. Rejecting the advertising category in our banner stops these cookies being set on this site in the first place.

Global Privacy Control

If your browser or an extension sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as a rejection of advertising cookies and, for California residents, as an opt out of sale and sharing. You do not need to do anything else, and the banner will still let you accept explicitly if you want to.

Controlling cookies in your browser

Every major browser lets you block or delete cookies. Blocking strictly necessary cookies will stop the cart and checkout working, which is a limitation of how carts work rather than a choice we made. Blocking everything else has no effect on your ability to browse, read or order.

  • Chrome: Settings, then Privacy and security, then Third-party cookies.
  • Safari: Settings, then Privacy.
  • Firefox: Settings, then Privacy & Security.
  • Edge: Settings, then Cookies and site permissions.

When this list changes

If we add or remove a cookie, this page changes on the same day and the date at the top updates. If a new category is introduced, the consent banner reappears and asks again rather than assuming your previous answer carries over.
