Getting it to you

Shipping and delivery

We ship within the fifty United States only, in US dollars, with tracking on every parcel and no handling fees added at checkout.

Free over $125 Flat $8 below Out within two business days Tracking on every parcel Fifty states, USD

The short version

Free over $125

Orders of $125 or more ship free anywhere in the fifty states. The threshold is on the order subtotal before any tax.

Flat $8 below that

One flat rate regardless of weight or box size. A five pound overcoat and a belt cost the same to send.

Out within two days

Dispatch within two business days of the order, usually the next morning. Four in the week after a drop.

Who carries it

Standard ground goes by USPS Ground Advantage for anything under two pounds and by UPS Ground for anything heavier, because it is cheaper and faster that way and we pass the choice on rather than charging a blended rate. Expedited air goes by UPS 2nd Day Air.

You do not choose the carrier at checkout. You choose the service, and we pick whichever carrier gets that service to your address for the least money. The tracking email tells you which one it went with.

Where it goes

All fifty states, the District of Columbia, and APO, FPO and DPO addresses. Alaska and Hawaii are included in the free shipping threshold and in the flat rate, with a longer transit window of five to ten business days.

We do not currently ship to US territories outside those listed, and we do not ship internationally. Every price on the site is in US dollars.

When it leaves

Orders placed before 2:00 p.m. Pacific Time on a business day are normally packed the same afternoon and collected the following morning. Orders placed after that, or at a weekend, go out on the next business day.

The exception is drop week. A drop goes live at 11:00 a.m. Pacific Time on the first Friday of the month and generates several days of concentrated volume. During that week we commit to four business days rather than two, and we say so at checkout rather than quietly missing the normal window.

Tracking

A tracking number is emailed the moment a label is created, which is usually a few hours before the parcel is actually collected. If tracking shows no movement for 24 hours after you receive that email, nothing is wrong. If it shows no movement for four business days, write to us and we will chase it.

In the box

What arrives

Orders ship in a plain unbleached kraft box, or a paper mailer for a belt or a cap. Inside, the garment is wrapped in uncoated tissue and secured with paper tape. Both the box and the tissue are recyclable in ordinary US curbside programmes.

There is no plastic polybag, no printed plastic sticker and no branded filler. The one plastic component is the shipping label itself, which is a thermal label with a plastic face because carrier systems require it, and we have not found a workable alternative.

The printed record card for the piece goes in with every order: the register number, the measurements, the fibre content, the condition notes and the dates of cleaning and repair. There is no flyer, no discount insert and no sample.

Sales tax

Sales tax is calculated at checkout based on your delivery address and is shown before you confirm the order. Nothing is added afterward.

Exceptions

When something goes wrong

  • Delayed in transit

    Carriers occasionally sit on a parcel for a few days without a scan. If there has been no movement for four business days, write to us with your order number and we will open a trace.

  • Marked delivered, not there

    Wait 48 hours and check with neighbours and building management. Parcels marked delivered turn up more often than they are stolen. After 48 hours we refund in full or substitute an equivalent piece.

  • Damaged in transit

    Photograph the box and the contents before unpacking further and send us both. We file the carrier claim and refund or replace immediately rather than waiting for the claim to resolve.

  • Wrong address entered

    Tell us within two hours and we can usually stop the label. After dispatch we will help you redirect through the carrier, though whether that works is out of our hands.

Because every piece is a single unit, a lost parcel usually cannot be replaced with the same garment. We say so honestly at the point it happens rather than promising a replacement we cannot make.
