Gift cards

Give the register, not a guess

Buying somebody a piece that exists in exactly one size is a gamble. A card that never expires is not.

The terms, in full

  • No expiry. Vestiary gift cards never expire. There is no dormancy fee, no maintenance fee and no reduction in value over time.
  • Partial use is fine. Any unused balance stays on the card and can be spent across as many orders as you like.
  • Stackable. A gift card can be combined with referral credit and store credit on the same order.
  • Not redeemable for cash except where state law requires it. Several US states require cash redemption of small remaining balances, and we honour those laws where they apply to you.
  • Not returnable. The card itself cannot be returned or refunded, though anything bought with it carries the usual 30-day return window. A refund on a gift-card purchase goes back to the card.
  • Delivered by email on the date you choose, with a short message if you want one. Nothing is posted, so nothing arrives late.
  • Replaceable. If the email goes astray, tell us and we will resend it. Codes are tied to the recipient address.

Gift cards are issued by Vestiary Supply Co. and are valid only against orders placed on this site. They cannot be used at stockists.

Ordering one

Email us the amount, the recipient's name and email address, the delivery date and any message you want included. We send a payment link, and the card goes out on the date you named.

If you would rather the recipient not know the amount, say so and we will leave it off the email. They will see the balance when they redeem it.

When you do not know their size

This is the most common reason people buy a card here, and it is the right instinct. If you would rather give an actual garment, there is a middle path: send us the flat measurements of something the recipient already wears, and we will suggest two or three pieces that would fit. You buy one, and if it is wrong they exchange it for anything else in the register with shipping covered both ways.

Referrals

Word of mouth, paid in credit

Share a link. When somebody orders through it for the first time and keeps what they bought, you both get store credit.

  • Ask us for your link

    Email us and we generate a personal referral link tied to your address. There is nothing to sign up for and no account required.

  • Share it with people you know

    The programme is built for word of mouth. We do not accept referral links posted to coupon aggregators or deal forums, and links used that way are closed.

  • They order, and it ships

    The referred person receives a store credit on their first order, applied at checkout.

  • Your credit follows the return window

    Thirty days after their order is delivered, a $20 store credit is added to your account. The wait exists so a returned order does not generate a credit for a sale that did not happen.

Referral terms

  • Amounts. $20 store credit for you, $20 off for the person you refer, on a first order of $75 or more.
  • Timing. Their discount applies at checkout. Your credit arrives 30 days after delivery, once the return window has closed.
  • First orders only. The referred person must be new to Vestiary and must not have ordered before under any address.
  • No self-referral. Links cannot be used on your own orders or by anyone at the same delivery address.
  • Store credit, not cash. Credits are not redeemable for cash and cannot be transferred between people.
  • No expiry. Referral credit does not expire and stacks with gift cards.
  • Stated limits. A maximum of ten referral credits per person per calendar year. We will tell you when you are close rather than silently stopping.
  • If the referred order is returned in full, no credit is issued. We will email to explain rather than leaving you wondering.

We reserve the right to close a referral link that is being used in ways this page does not describe, and we will always explain why by email before doing it.

What we do not run

No affiliate programme

We pay no commission to bloggers, publishers or content sites. Nothing on this website is an affiliate link, and nothing we publish is sponsored.

No points scheme

There is no tiered loyalty programme, no points balance and no status level. The register is too small for any of that to mean anything.

No urgency on credits

Credits never expire and we will never send an email telling you that they are about to. If we ever change that policy, existing credits will be honoured under the old terms.

In short

Nothing here runs out

A gift card holds its value indefinitely and so does a referral credit. Neither carries a fee, neither shrinks with time, and neither will ever be the subject of a reminder email telling you to hurry. The only clock on this page is the thirty-day return window that a referral credit waits for.
