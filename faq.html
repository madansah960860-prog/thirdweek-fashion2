The register, answered

Questions we get

Twenty-one questions, answered in full and kept current. If yours is missing, write to us and it will most likely be on this page next month.

Free US shipping over $125 Flat $8 below 30-day returns One garment. One size. One owner next.

Section one

Fit and condition

Every garment is measured flat, in inches, against a steel rule, and the US size follows from those numbers. Between five and nine measurements go onto a record card before the piece is photographed on the plate, and that card becomes the listing.

The original label is plated separately so you can read it, but it does not set the size. Sizing conventions have drifted upward for fifty years, differently in every country and every category, so a 1979 sweater marked medium frequently measures smaller than a modern small. Quoting you the old label would only pass that problem on.

Excellent. No visible flaws under a strong lamp. Ribs elastic, seams sound, colour even. It may still show that it has been worn.

Very good. Minor wear consistent with age, such as slight fading at a cuff or light pilling at an underarm. Nothing that affects how the garment functions.

Good. Visible wear or a specific named flaw. A paint mark, a repaired belt loop, a darn on an elbow. Each one is photographed and written into the condition line in plain words.

There is no grade below good. A piece that would fall into one does not get listed.

Measure a garment you already own that fits the way you want it to, then compare its flat chest against ours. That comparison is far more reliable than body measurements, and it matters most on outerwear cut to go over a jacket.

Still unsure, send us the numbers and the register number. We will tell you honestly whether it will work, including when the answer is no. We have talked people out of pieces more than once, and it is a better outcome than a return.

We list what we find, and secondhand supply above US XL is genuinely thin, particularly in older wool. When it does turn up, it goes straight into the wardrobe.

The want list is the more reliable route. Tell us the category, the flat chest measurement you need and the fabric you prefer, and we look at every intake. There is no charge and no obligation to buy. Around a quarter of our want list matches are XL and above.

Because the measurement disagrees with the label, and we trust the measurement. The gap is normal, and it usually tells you roughly which decade a garment comes from. Trust the numbers on the record card.

Yes. Every flaw and every repair is photographed and named. We do not retouch the plates, we do not clean the edge shadow out of them, and we do not dye or paint leather to hide wear. Find something on a garment that is not in its record and that is a mis-description: we pay return shipping both ways.

Section two

Orders, shipping, returns

Free within the fifty United States on orders of $125 or more. Below that it is a flat $8, whatever the weight or the size of the box, so a five pound overcoat and a belt cost the same to send.

Expedited air shipping is offered at checkout at cost. It is never preselected and it is never free. The full detail sits on the shipping page.

Within two business days of the order, and usually the next morning. The exception is the first week of the month, immediately after a drop, when it can stretch to four business days on volume. Tracking is emailed as soon as the label is created.

Yes, within 30 days of delivery, in the condition it arrived in and with the record card included.

We pay return shipping on exchanges and on anything we described wrongly. On a change-of-mind refund, an $8 return label fee comes out of the refund. The sequence is set out step by step on the returns page.

Not for the same garment in another size, because there is no other size. What we can do is exchange it for any other piece in the register, and we pay shipping in both directions on that. If the new piece costs more you pay the difference. If it costs less we refund it.

Not at the moment. Every price on the site is in US dollars and every shipping option is domestic. We would rather say no than take an international order we cannot service properly on duties and returns.

If that changes, it will be announced in the drop email and on the shipping page first.

Tell us and we open a claim with the carrier the same day. If tracking shows delivered and the parcel is not there, we wait 48 hours, because parcels marked delivered turn up at a neighbour more often than they are stolen. After that we refund in full or replace with something of equal value from the register, your choice.

Because pieces are one of a kind, a lost parcel usually cannot be replaced with the same garment. We are honest about that from the start.

Section three

The register, drops, Re-cut

No. Every register number belongs to exactly one garment. When it sells the listing closes and the number retires permanently. We run no waitlists on sold pieces, because there is nothing to wait for.

What we do run is a want list by category, size and fabric. That is a genuine search rather than a queue.

Drops go live at 11:00 a.m. Pacific Time on the first Friday of each month. Confirmed dates are published in advance on the new arrivals page, and there is no countdown timer anywhere on this site.

If a date has to move, it changes on that page first and in the drop email second, with the reason.

Forty-eight hours. In a single-run wardrobe a hold means nobody else can buy the piece, so we keep the window short and we do not extend it except where a bank transfer is genuinely in transit.

Made-to-order pieces cut from garments too damaged to sell whole. Vests, caps, mittens and patch overshirts, sewn by a seamstress in Milwaukie, Oregon.

Lead times are two to three weeks for caps and mittens, four to six for a vest and six to eight for an overshirt. Those are the times we actually hit rather than a best case, and if an order is going to run past its range we email before the range expires and offer a full refund.

From four inputs: what we paid at intake, the direct cost of cleaning and repair, the labour of measuring and plating, and scarcity in that size. Not on brand and not on decade.

There is no markdown calendar. If a piece has not sold in ninety days we re-photograph it and reconsider the price once, permanently. No sale events, no flash discounts.

Because in a single-run wardrobe a product review is structurally misleading. Nobody reading a review of VS-0431 can ever buy VS-0431, so the review tells them nothing about what they would receive.

If we add reviews later they will be about service rather than products, clearly labelled as such, and verified against real orders. We will not publish anything we have not verified.

Section four

Cards, referrals, accounts

No. Vestiary gift cards never expire and carry no dormancy or maintenance fees. They are redeemable against anything on the site including made-to-order pieces, and any unused balance stays on the card. Full terms are on the gift cards page.

You share a personal link. When somebody orders through it for the first time, both of you receive a store credit once that order has shipped and cleared the 30-day return window. The wait exists so that a returned order does not generate a credit for a sale that did not happen.

Credits are store credit, not cash, and they stack with gift cards. The gift cards and referrals page has the full terms.

No. Guest checkout is available on every order and we do not push you toward an account at any point. An account only stores your address and your order history so you do not have to type them again.

You can ask us to delete an account and its data at any time, and we will confirm within ten business days. See the Privacy Policy and the Do Not Sell or Share page for your rights under California law.

One more thing

Ask us directly

A person reads every message. Two business days at the outside, usually the same day.

Nothing on this page is a script. If an answer here does not settle the question you actually have, send the register number and what you need to know. Measurements, a closer plate of a flaw, the weight of a cloth: we will look at the garment and write back with the number rather than a guess.
