Privacy

What we hold, and why

Last updated September 8, 2026 · Applies to www.vestiary.co

Vestiary Supply Co. takes a small amount of information to send a garment, answer a question and keep the site standing up. This policy states what that information is, who else sees it, how long it stays on the record, and what you can do about all of it.

What this page covers Collection Cookies Vendors Retention Your rights Contact

Twelve numbered sections follow, in this order: what we hold, why we use it and on what basis, cookies and consent, the third parties involved, how long each record survives, your rights as a United States resident, children, visitors from Europe and the United Kingdom, marketing email, security, how changes are announced, and where to write. The framing here is new. The commitments underneath it are the ones we have always made, and the sections below govern.

Who you are dealing with. Vestiary Supply Co., 218 NW Couch Street, Suite 4A, Portland, OR 97209, United States. Email privacy@vestiary.co. Telephone (503) 555-0192. We are the controller of the personal information described here.

1. What we hold

Given by you

  • Order information. Name, delivery address, billing address, email address and telephone number where you provide one.
  • Payment information. Card details are typed straight into our payment processor's form and are never stored on our systems. What reaches us is the last four digits, the card brand and an authorisation result.
  • Correspondence. Anything you send through the contact form, the wholesale form or by email, including any photographs you attach.
  • Newsletter signups. Your email address and the date and source of your consent.
  • Want list entries. The categories, sizes and fabrics you ask us to look for.
  • Account details if you choose to create one. An account is optional and guest checkout is available for every order.

Collected automatically

  • Server logs. IP address, browser user agent, referring page, the pages requested and the time of the request. The hosting infrastructure generates these for every visit.
  • Strictly necessary cookies. A session identifier for the shopping cart and a record of your cookie choices. Both are set without consent because the site cannot function without them.
  • Analytics data, only if you accept analytics cookies. Aggregate page views, referrers and device categories.
  • Advertising data, only if you accept advertising cookies. See section 4.

Never collected

We do not collect biometric data, precise geolocation, government identifiers, health information or any special category data. We do not buy personal information from data brokers, and we do not build behavioural profiles of visitors.

3. Cookies and consent

A cookie banner appears on your first visit and offers three choices: accept all, reject non-essential, or manage preferences by category. No analytics or advertising cookies are set before you make that choice. Only the strictly necessary cookies described above are set beforehand, because the cart and the consent record itself depend on them.

You can change your choice at any time using the cookie settings link in the footer of every page. The Cookie Policy lists each cookie, its purpose, who sets it and how long it lasts.

We honour the Global Privacy Control signal. If your browser or extension sends a GPC header, we treat it as an opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for California residents, and we do not set advertising cookies for that session.

4. Who else sees it, including Google

The vendor list is short. Each one receives only what it needs to do its job.

  • Payment processor. Handles card data directly. We never see or store full card numbers.
  • Shipping carriers. Receive the delivery name, address and telephone number in order to deliver the parcel.
  • Email service provider. Stores newsletter addresses and sends drop notices.
  • Hosting provider. Operates the servers and generates the access logs described above.
  • Analytics provider. Receives aggregate usage data, only with your consent.
  • Google. If advertising is served on this site and you have accepted advertising cookies, Google and its partners may set and read cookies on your device.

Google and advertising cookies

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by Google by visiting Google Ads Settings. You may opt out of personalised advertising from many other vendors at optout.aboutads.info and optout.networkadvertising.org. Rejecting advertising cookies in our banner also prevents them being set here.

Google's own handling of data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. We do not control it and we do not receive the personal data Google collects.

5. How long each record survives

  • Order records: seven years, because US tax and accounting rules require it.
  • Correspondence: three years from the last message in a thread, then deleted.
  • Newsletter subscriptions: until you unsubscribe, plus a suppression record of your email address kept indefinitely so we do not accidentally add you again.
  • Want list entries: eighteen months, then deleted unless you ask us to renew them.
  • Server logs: ninety days.
  • Analytics data: fourteen months in aggregate form.
  • Cookie consent records: twelve months, after which the banner appears again.

6. Your rights in the United States

California: the CCPA and the CPRA

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act gives you the following rights:

  • Right to know what personal information we have collected, the sources, the purposes, and the categories of third parties it was disclosed to.
  • Right to delete personal information we hold about you, subject to legal retention requirements such as tax records.
  • Right to correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, including sharing for cross-context behavioural advertising.
  • Right to limit the use of sensitive personal information. We do not collect sensitive personal information as defined by the statute.
  • Right to non-discrimination. Exercising any of these rights will never change the price you pay or the service you receive.

Do we sell personal information? We do not sell personal information for money. If you accept advertising cookies, the resulting disclosure to advertising vendors may constitute "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising under the CPRA. You can prevent that by rejecting advertising cookies, by sending a Global Privacy Control signal, or by using our Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information page.

To exercise any right, email privacy@vestiary.co or write to the postal address at the top of this policy. We confirm receipt within ten business days and respond substantively within forty-five days, extendable once by a further forty-five days where the request is complex. You may use an authorised agent, in which case we will ask for written proof of authorisation.

Other states

Residents of Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah, Texas, Oregon, Montana and other states with comprehensive privacy laws have broadly similar rights of access, correction, deletion, portability and opt out of targeted advertising. We apply the same process to all of them and do not require you to prove your state of residence before we act.

7. Children

This site is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, in accordance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act. We do not direct any part of this website to children, we do not run advertising aimed at children, and we do not have any feature designed to appeal to them.

If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, email privacy@vestiary.co and we will delete it and any associated account within five business days. A parent or guardian may also request access to, or deletion of, information we hold about their child.

8. Visitors from Europe and the United Kingdom

We ship only within the United States, but this website can be reached from anywhere. If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom and you visit this site, the General Data Protection Regulation and the UK GDPR give you rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, together with the right to withdraw consent at any time and the right to lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority.

Our servers and vendors are located in the United States, so visiting this site involves a transfer of your data to the US. Where we rely on a vendor to process personal data, that vendor is bound by a data processing agreement incorporating the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Because we do not offer goods or services to individuals in the EEA or the UK, and do not monitor behaviour there beyond ordinary web analytics you can decline, our processing of EEA and UK visitor data is limited to server logs and any cookies you affirmatively accept.

9. Marketing email and CAN-SPAM

Marketing email is sent only to people who have opted in. Every message we send identifies Vestiary Supply Co. as the sender, carries our physical mailing address, and contains a working unsubscribe link. Unsubscribes are actioned immediately and in all cases within ten business days, as the CAN-SPAM Act requires. We do not use deceptive subject lines or forged header information.

Transactional email about an order you have placed is not marketing and will still be sent after you unsubscribe from the newsletter, because we cannot ship you a parcel without telling you it is coming.

10. Keeping it secure

The site is served over HTTPS. Payment card data never touches our systems. Access to order records is limited to the three people who work here, each with an individual account and multi-factor authentication. Backups are encrypted at rest.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal information we will notify you and the relevant authorities within the timeframes required by applicable state law.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top and, for any material change, place a notice on the site for thirty days and email newsletter subscribers. Previous versions are available on request.

12. Where to write about data

Email privacy@vestiary.co, call (503) 555-0192 during Pacific Time business hours, or write to Vestiary Supply Co., 218 NW Couch Street, Suite 4A, Portland, OR 97209, United States. Mark data requests for the attention of the privacy contact.

You do not have to cite a statute. There is no form to fill in and no wording we require. Write to privacy@vestiary.co in plain sentences, say what you want done, and we will work out which right applies and tell you what we did with it. Asking costs you nothing and changes nothing about your prices, your shipping or your return rights.
