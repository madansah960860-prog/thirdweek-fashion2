Bridleworks Leather Belt

Register VS-0425 · 1990s · US 34 · one unit only

$58

A 1.5 inch belt cut from a single piece of English bridle leather, with a solid nickel roller buckle and edges burnished at the bench rather than painted. Bridle leather is vegetable tanned and then stuffed with tallow and wax, which is why it darkens instead of cracking, and why a belt like this outlasts several pairs of the trousers it holds up.

The flesh side is where a belt tells the truth. Cheap belts are two thin layers glued together and the seam shows along the edge. This is one solid strap, roughly four millimetres thick. View three is the underside, so you can check that claim yourself before you buy it.

How it was prepared

Cleaned, conditioned, left honest

The strap was cleaned and conditioned at the bench in August 2026 with a neutral cream, worked in and buffed off. No dye. No edge paint. No attempt to hide wear. The hole creasing at the second and third holes stayed where a previous owner put it, and the light scuff 6 in from the tip was left alone, because both are visible in the plates and neither affects how the belt works.

Thickness was taken at three points along the strap at roughly 4 mm. The rivets at the buckle fold were pushed and pulled and did not move, and no hole showed cracking. It was then measured on a steel rule at 34 in from the base of the buckle to the centre hole, 40 in overall, and plated coiled across four views.

Leather and make

  • Leather Full-grain English bridle, vegetable tanned, tallow and wax stuffed
  • Thickness Approximately 4 mm, measured at three points along the strap
  • Buckle Solid nickel roller buckle, 1.5 in, no plating and therefore no plating loss
  • Attachment Strap folded at the buckle and secured with two solid brass rivets, no stitching to fail
  • Edges Burnished and slicked by hand, not painted. Darkened to near black with handling
  • Holes Five, hand punched, evenly spaced at 1 in centres

Measured on the rule

  • Assigned size US 34, measured from the base of the buckle to the centre hole
  • Comfortable range 32 in at the tightest hole to 36 in at the loosest
  • Width 1.5 in, which fits standard jean and chino belt loops
  • Total length 40 in including the buckle
  • Tip Pointed, 3.5 in of tail beyond the last hole when worn at the centre
  • Rule of thumb Belt size usually runs two inches above trouser waist size

Care

  • After wear Wipe with a dry cloth. Do not soak or submerge
  • Condition Twice a year with a neutral leather cream, worked in with a finger and buffed off
  • Never Do not use silicone, mink oil or shoe polish. All three will darken the strap unevenly
  • Storage Hang it or lay it flat. Rolling tightly for months sets a permanent curve
  • If it gets wet Let it dry at room temperature away from any heat source
  • Extra holes Any cobbler can punch them in a few minutes for very little

Sizing

How belt sizes are measured

Belt sizes are quoted from the base of the buckle to the centre hole, in inches. Measure a belt you already wear, not your waistband.

Between two sizes, take the larger one. A belt that is too long can be shortened at the buckle end by any cobbler for a small charge, and the buckle goes back on the shortened strap. A belt that is too short cannot be lengthened at all.
