The form

Send us a message

Every field is required except the reference number and the phone field. For a fit question, the single most useful thing you can send is the flat measurements of a garment you already own that fits you the way you want.

Only if you would rather we called than emailed.

Plate numbers look like VS-0431. Order numbers look like VS-10428.
At least ten characters. Measurements, dates and photographs all help.

Before you send

Before you write

What to include

  • Fit questions. The flat chest, length and sleeve of a garment you own that fits you the way you want, plus the plate number you are considering.
  • Order questions. Your order number and the email address you used at checkout.
  • Returns. Order number, plate number and one sentence on the reason. You do not need to justify a change of mind.
  • Want list. Category, US size range, fabric preference and a rough budget. No obligation and no charge.
  • Wholesale. Shop name, city, the categories you carry and your usual price band. The stockists page has a dedicated form.
  • Data requests. Say which right you are exercising and we will confirm within ten business days.

How long we take

One business day is the aim and two is the commitment. In the week of a drop, the first week of each month, that stretches to three because everybody is packing boxes. If three business days pass with nothing from us, something has gone wrong with the email and it is worth calling.

What we cannot do

We cannot hold a piece indefinitely without payment, because in a one-of-one register a hold means somebody else cannot buy it. A reservation lasts 48 hours. We also cannot source a specific branded garment on request. The want list works on category, size and fabric, not on labels.

Routing

Press and wholesale

Press enquiries, image requests and journal correspondence go to press@vestiary.co, and we answer them on the same two business day clock as everything else.

Shops go to the wholesale enquiry form rather than to email, because it asks for the ZIP code and price band we need before we can reply usefully. Those get a response within three business days.

Privacy and data rights requests go to privacy@vestiary.co, or use the privacy subject on the form. Either way we confirm within ten business days.
