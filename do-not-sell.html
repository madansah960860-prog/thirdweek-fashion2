Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

Last updated September 8, 2026 · California Consumer Privacy Act and California Privacy Rights Act

We do not sell personal information for money and never have. If you accept advertising cookies, the resulting disclosure to advertising vendors may count as "sharing" under California law. This page is how you stop that.

What this page covers Three opt-out routes GPC honoured No verification needed No change to price or service

The three routes below are equivalent. Any one of them is enough on its own, and you do not have to explain yourself to use one. After them: exactly what we do with your information today, what changes the moment you opt out, the other California rights you hold alongside this one, how to make a request and how we verify it, how authorised agents work, and a note for residents of other states.

Three ways to opt out, any of which works on its own

1. Reject advertising cookies

Open the cookie settings and switch the advertising category off, or choose reject non-essential. This takes effect immediately on this browser.

Open cookie settings

2. Send a Global Privacy Control signal

We honour GPC. If your browser or extension sends the header, we treat it as an opt out automatically and you do not need to tell us anything.

How to enable GPC

3. Email us

A request by email applies to your customer record across every device rather than just this browser.

Email the request

What we actually do with your information

To be specific rather than reassuring:

  • We do not sell personal information for monetary consideration. No customer list has ever been sold, rented or licensed, and we do not intend to sell one.
  • We do not disclose personal information to data brokers.
  • We do share limited information with service providers who need it to do a job for us: a payment processor, shipping carriers, an email provider and our host. Those are service provider relationships under the CCPA, contractually restricted to our instructions, and they are not sales.
  • Advertising cookies are the exception. This site carries labelled advertising slots. If you accept the advertising category, vendors including Google may set cookies that enable cross-context behavioural advertising. The CPRA treats that as "sharing" whether or not money changes hands, which is exactly why this page exists.

What happens after you opt out

Advertising cookies stop being set on this site. Any that already exist on your device from us are no longer read by our advertising slots, and you can delete them in your browser at any time. You may still see advertising, but it will not be personalised using data collected here.

Opting out changes nothing else. Your prices, shipping rates, return rights and service are identical. The CCPA prohibits discrimination for exercising a privacy right and we would not do it anyway.

An opt out made through the cookie banner or GPC is stored per browser, so you will need to repeat it on a different device or after clearing cookies. An opt out made by email is applied to your customer record and persists.

Your other California rights

Alongside the right to opt out of sale and sharing, California residents have the right to know what we collect and why, the right to delete, the right to correct inaccurate information, the right to limit the use of sensitive personal information, and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising any of them. We do not collect sensitive personal information as the statute defines it.

All of these are explained in full, with the categories of information involved, in the Privacy Policy.

Making a request

Email privacy@vestiary.co, call (503) 555-0192, or write to Vestiary Supply Co., 218 NW Couch Street, Suite 4A, Portland, OR 97209, United States.

Tell us which right you are exercising and the email address associated with any orders. We confirm receipt within ten business days and respond substantively within forty-five days, extendable once by a further forty-five days if the request is complex, in which case we will tell you why before the first period runs out.

To verify a request we match the details you give us against our order records. We ask for the minimum needed and we do not create an account or collect anything new in order to verify you. An opt out of sale or sharing does not require verification at all, so we action it straight away.

Authorised agents

You may use an authorised agent to submit a request. We will ask for written permission signed by you, and we may contact you to confirm. An agent submitting an opt out under GPC on your behalf does not need to provide that documentation.

Residents of other states

Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah, Texas, Oregon, Montana and other states with comprehensive privacy laws provide a right to opt out of targeted advertising. This page works for you too, and we do not require proof of residency before honouring a request.

The quiet version of all of this. Opting out is a switch, not a negotiation. Nobody reads your request looking for a reason to refuse it, no one asks why, and the answer is never a discount offer. Flip the advertising category off, or send one line to privacy@vestiary.co, and the matter is closed.
