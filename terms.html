The terms of a sale

Last updated September 8, 2026 · Applies to www.vestiary.co

These terms govern your use of this website and any purchase you make from it. Placing an order means you accept them. They are written to be readable, and nothing in them is intended to remove a right you have under US consumer protection law.

What this page covers Sixteen sections Free US shipping over $125 30-day returns Priced in USD

Sixteen numbered sections follow: who may order, what one-of-one stock means for a contract, how we describe condition, when an order is accepted, price and tax, shipping and risk, what happens to a lost parcel, returns, made-to-order work, gift cards and referral credit, acceptable use of the site, warranty and liability, accessibility and errors, how these terms change, governing law, and where to write. The shipping page and the returns page are named below and form part of these terms.

1. Who can order

You must be at least 18 years old and able to enter a binding contract. We ship only to addresses within the fifty United States, the District of Columbia and APO, FPO and DPO addresses. All prices are in US dollars.

2. One garment, one owner next

Every item is a single used garment identified by a plate number such as VS-0431. There is no second unit, no restock and no equivalent replacement. When an item sells, its listing closes and the plate number retires permanently.

Because of this, we cannot exchange an item for the same item in a different size, and a lost or damaged parcel usually cannot be replaced with the same garment. Section 7 explains what happens instead.

3. Condition and description

All items are secondhand and sold as used goods. Signs of previous wear, fading, creasing and repair are inherent to what we sell and are not defects.

Every item is described from our own inspection: fibre content, flat measurements in inches, condition grade, named flaws and any work we carried out. Photographs are unretouched plates. Colour reproduction is close but not exact, and where a colour renders poorly we say so on the record.

If an item arrives materially different from its description, that is a mis-description. Tell us and we will pay shipping in both directions and refund in full. This right sits alongside, and does not replace, any statutory right you have.

4. Orders and acceptance

Your order is an offer. A contract forms when we send the dispatch confirmation, not when you receive the order confirmation. We may decline an order, and will always explain why, if the item has already sold, if the listing carried an obvious pricing error, if the delivery address falls outside our shipping area, or if payment authorisation fails.

Reservations last 48 hours from the time we confirm them. After that the item returns to general availability without further notice, because holding a single unit indefinitely prevents anyone else buying it.

5. Prices, tax and payment

Prices are in US dollars and exclude sales tax, which is calculated at checkout from your delivery address and shown before you confirm. Nothing is added after you confirm.

We do not run sale events, flash discounts or time-limited pricing. If an item has not sold in ninety days we may reconsider its price once, permanently. A price change of that kind is not a promotion and does not entitle earlier buyers to a partial refund.

Payment is taken at the point of order for stocked items, and at the point of cutting for made-to-order Re-cut pieces. Card data is handled entirely by our payment processor.

6. Shipping and risk

Shipping within the fifty states is free on orders of $125 or more and a flat $8 below that. Full rates, carriers and timings are on the shipping page and form part of these terms.

Delivery estimates are estimates. Risk of loss passes to you on delivery to the address you provided, but see section 7 for what we do when a parcel goes missing.

7. Lost, delayed and damaged parcels

If tracking shows no movement for four business days, tell us and we will open a carrier trace. If a parcel is marked delivered but is not there, we wait 48 hours and then refund in full or substitute an item of equal value, your choice. If a parcel arrives damaged, send photographs of the box and the contents and we refund or replace immediately, without waiting for the carrier claim to resolve.

8. Returns and exchanges

You may return or exchange within 30 days of delivery, in the condition the item arrived in, with the record card included. We pay return shipping on exchanges and on anything we described wrongly. On a change-of-mind refund an $8 prepaid label fee is deducted.

Items altered at your request before shipping are final sale, and we confirm that in writing before carrying out the alteration. Items laundered or altered after delivery cannot be returned. The full process is on the returns page, which forms part of these terms.

9. Made to order

Re-cut pieces are cut from salvaged cloth to a pattern and size you choose. Because each piece comes from a different garment, exact colour and cloth cannot be guaranteed, and you approve the specific salvage before we cut.

Published lead times are two to eight weeks depending on the pattern. If an order will run past the top of its stated range, we contact you before the range expires and offer a full refund. Made-to-order pieces carry the same 30-day return window as everything else.

10. Gift cards and referral credit

Gift cards never expire and carry no fees. They are not redeemable for cash except where state law requires redemption of a small remaining balance, and they cannot be used at stockists. Gift cards themselves are not returnable, though items bought with one carry the usual return rights and refund to the card.

Referral credit is store credit, is not transferable and is not redeemable for cash. It is issued after the referred order has been delivered and has cleared the 30-day return window. Self-referral, referral of another person at the same delivery address, and posting referral links to coupon or deal aggregators are outside the programme, and we may close a link used that way after telling you why. Full terms are on the gift cards and referrals page.

11. Using this website

You may browse, read, print and share this site freely. You may not scrape it at a rate that degrades service for other visitors, republish our photography or record-card text commercially, or use the site to send unsolicited commercial messages.

All original photography, record card text, journal articles and site design are the property of Vestiary Supply Co. Product names of the original manufacturers, where mentioned in a record for the purpose of accurate description, remain the property of their respective owners and no affiliation is implied.

12. Warranty and liability

Items are sold as used goods in the condition described. To the fullest extent permitted by law, and except for our express commitments in sections 3, 7 and 8, we disclaim implied warranties of merchantability and fitness for a particular purpose.

Our total liability arising from any order is limited to the amount you paid for that order. We are not liable for indirect or consequential losses. Nothing in this section limits liability for fraud, for personal injury caused by negligence, or for anything else that cannot lawfully be limited.

13. Accessibility and errors

We aim to meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA and welcome reports of anything that fails. See the Accessibility Statement. If a page contains an obvious factual or typographical error, particularly in a measurement or a price, we correct it and honour the corrected version.

14. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms. The version in force for your order is the one published when the order was placed, and we keep dated previous versions available on request. Material changes are announced on the site for thirty days.

15. Governing law and disputes

These terms are governed by the laws of the State of Oregon, without regard to conflict of law rules. Any dispute will be brought in the state or federal courts located in Multnomah County, Oregon, and both parties consent to that jurisdiction.

Before litigating anything, please write to us. Every dispute we have had so far has been resolved by email within a week.
