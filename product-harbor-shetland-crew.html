Harbor Shetland Crewneck

Register VS-0428 · 1980s · US M · one unit only

$96

A woollen-spun Shetland crewneck in oatmeal heather, flecked with grey and a little rust. Woollen spinning leaves the fibre lofted rather than combed smooth, which is why a true Shetland feels slightly hairy in the hand and then settles against a shirt over the first few wears. It holds a surprising amount of air for its weight and stays warm when damp. That is the whole reason the construction survived into the twentieth century.

This one came from a shop clearance in Corvallis in June 2026. It had been stored folded in a cedar chest, which explains the cuffs and hem still being fully elastic and the absence of moth grazing anywhere on it.

Condition, in full

The grade is excellent, which on a forty-year-old sweater means the inspection matters more than the adjective. Both underarms, the neckband and all four seams were checked under a lamp. No moth grazing. No pilling, no thinning, no darns, no odour. The 2 x 2 rib measures 2.5 in at the cuff and 2 in at the hem and both still snap back, which is the first thing to go on a wool crew that has been hung rather than folded.

It was hand washed in July 2026 in cool water, rolled in a towel, dried flat and reshaped to 21 in across the chest while damp. It was then weighed on the bench at 1 lb 3 oz, the gauge was counted twice on the front body at 7 stitches per inch, and it was plated folded in July 2026 across four views.

Fibre and make

  • Fibre 100% Shetland wool, woollen spun, undyed base with dyed blend fibres
  • Gauge 7 stitches per inch over two inches, counted twice on the front body
  • Construction Raglan sleeve, fully fashioned decreases visible at the shoulder line
  • Neck Hand-linked crew neckband, 1 in deep, no rolling
  • Cuffs and hem 2 x 2 rib, 2.5 in at the cuff, 2 in at the hem, both fully elastic
  • Weight 1 lb 3 oz on the bench

Measured flat

  • Chest, flat 21 in, one inch below the armhole, so 42 in around
  • Body length 26 in from the shoulder seam at the neck to the hem
  • Sleeve 33 in from the centre back of the neck to the cuff
  • Cuff opening 3.5 in flat, stretching comfortably to 5 in
  • Cut Straight through the body with no drop shoulder
  • Layering An oxford shirt sits under it without pulling at the placket. A flannel shirt is tight

Care

  • Wash Hand wash in cool water with a wool-specific detergent. No agitation and no wringing
  • Dry Roll in a dry towel and press the water out, then dry flat on a rack
  • Reshape Set it to 21 in across the chest while it is still damp. Wool keeps the shape it dries in
  • Never Do not hang it wet. It will grow two inches in the body and stay there
  • Between wears Air it overnight instead of washing. Three or four washes a year is plenty
  • Storage Store folded with cedar, not in plastic
