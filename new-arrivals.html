The calendar

Dates published before the clothes are

New pieces go on the register once a month, at 11:00 a.m. Pacific Time on the first Friday. The cadence follows the work: intake, laundry, repair, measuring and plating take about three weeks for a batch of seventy garments. We would rather fix a date and keep it than post whenever a box happens to be finished.

At 11:00 a.m. on that Friday the listings open. There is no countdown on this page and no timer anywhere on the site. The date is the date. If the wet cleaner runs late or a repair takes longer than planned, we move the date here first and explain it in the drop email. That has happened twice since we started, both times at the laundry.

Around sixty pieces sit outside the drop cycle altogether, listed permanently as the seasonless capsule. They return when we find another one, which is unpredictable by nature.

Re-cut

Cloth that outlives the garment

Some pieces arrive damaged past repair and still hold yards of sound fabric. Re-cut is where that fabric goes.

A wool overcoat with a shattered lining and moth damage through one front panel is finished as a coat. It is not finished as cloth. The back and sleeves usually carry enough undamaged wool for a vest, a cap and a pair of mittens. That is the Re-cut line, sewn with a seamstress in Milwaukie who has been cutting production patterns for twenty-six years.

Every piece comes off a different salvaged garment, so we cannot promise an exact colour or a specific cloth. We can promise the pattern, the size range and the lead time. You choose a pattern and a size, we show you which salvage cloths are in the bin, and you pick one before anything is cut.

Honest lead times

  • Vest, sizes XS to XL: 4 to 6 weeks from order
  • Cap, one size with an adjustable back: 2 to 3 weeks from order
  • Mittens, S, M and L: 2 to 3 weeks from order
  • Patch overshirt, sizes S to XL: 6 to 8 weeks from order

Those are working weeks, and they are the times we actually hit rather than a best case. If a Re-cut order is going to run past the top of its range, we email before the range expires and offer a full refund.

How a Re-cut order runs

  • Name a pattern and a size

    Write to us with the pattern and your US size. If you are unsure, send the flat measurements of something you already own and we will match against them.

  • Pick cloth that exists

    We send plates of the salvage currently in the bin, with the yardage available on each. You choose one. Nothing is cut until you confirm.

  • Pay on the day we cut

    Payment is taken the day the cloth is cut, which is also the day the lead time starts. Up to that point you can cancel at no charge.

  • Returns still apply

    Made-to-order pieces carry the same 30-day return window as everything else. We can usually recut a piece rather than refund it, but the choice is yours.
