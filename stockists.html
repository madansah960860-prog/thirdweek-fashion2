Stockists

Six shops, and the seventh

Six independent stores currently carry pieces from the register. If you run one and want to be the seventh, the terms and the form are at the foot of this page.

A single unit per piece makes this something other than a conventional wholesale relationship. There is no order sheet, no minimum quantity per style and no repeat ordering, because there are no styles to repeat. What a shop receives is a capsule of twenty to forty individual garments, chosen against that shop's price band and customer base, held on consignment for a fixed period.

Wholesale

Consignment, not reordering

Forty garments. Forty plate numbers.

A conventional wholesale account assumes you can reorder what sells. We cannot offer that, so the model is different, and we would rather set it out in full than have a shop discover its shape after signing.

Terms at a glance

A capsule of 20 to 40 pieces, held for 90 days, split 60 to the shop and 40 to us on an agreed retail price. We pay outbound freight. Invoices go out monthly on sold pieces, net 30. Prices are fixed for the term and record cards travel with every garment.

The terms

  • Format: a consigned capsule of 20 to 40 individual pieces, selected by us against your price band, climate and customer sizes.
  • Term: 90 days from delivery, extendable once by agreement.
  • Split: 60 percent to the shop, 40 percent to us, on the retail price we agree together before the capsule ships.
  • Freight: we pay outbound. The shop pays return freight on unsold pieces, or keeps them for a second term at no additional cost.
  • Records: every piece arrives with its printed record card carrying the measurements, fibre content and condition notes. Those cards are the point. Please display them.
  • Pricing: the retail price is fixed for the term. We do not allow markdown events on consigned stock, for the same reason we do not run them ourselves.
  • Reporting: a simple sold list by plate number at the end of each month, in whatever format suits your till system.
  • Payment: we invoice monthly on sold pieces, net 30.

What we look for in a shop

  • An independent store with a physical location, not a pop-up or a marketplace seller account.
  • Staff who will read a record card to a customer rather than guessing at a size.
  • A price band where a $96 sweater and a $265 overcoat both make sense.
  • Somewhere we would happily send a customer.

We work with six shops at present and expect to add two or three a year. A no is almost always because there is already a stockist within an hour of you, and we do not want two shops competing for the same forty garments.
