Disclaimer

What the record can and cannot tell you

Last updated September 8, 2026 · Applies to www.vestiary.co

Everything written on this site is written carefully, and none of it should carry more weight than it can bear. This page marks the edges: where a measurement becomes an estimate, where an opinion is not an authentication, and where advice is general rather than guaranteed.

What this page covers Measurements Colour Care Dating Advertising Affiliate links

Ten short sections follow. They deal with product information and measurement tolerance, colour and photography, care advice, dating and provenance, journal articles, links to other sites, advertising, affiliate links, the absence of professional advice, and site availability. Two of them state a plain negative that is worth reading in full: this site carries no advertising today, and it contains no affiliate links.

Product information

Every item is secondhand and sold as used goods. Measurements are taken by hand with a steel rule on a garment pressed flat, and are accurate to roughly a quarter of an inch. Condition grades reflect our own inspection under a strong lamp and are a judgement, not a certification.

Signs of previous wear, creasing, fading and repair are inherent to secondhand clothing and are not defects. Where we have named a flaw in a condition line, that is the flaw we found. If you receive an item with something material we did not describe, that is a mis-description and it is covered by the returns policy, not by this disclaimer.

Colour and photography

Images are unretouched plates, shot flat under raking light. Colour is reproduced as accurately as we can manage against a grey card, but our lights render some dyes poorly, particularly saturated reds and certain indigos, and every display shows colour differently. Where we know a colour renders badly, the record says so. Colour variation within those limits is not a mis-description.

Care instructions

Care instructions on this site are our own recommendations based on the fibre we identified and on experience with similar garments. They are general guidance, not a guarantee of outcome. Old textiles behave unpredictably, and factors we cannot see, such as an earlier dry cleaning or a home dye, can change how a garment responds to washing.

Test any treatment on an inconspicuous area first. We are not responsible for damage arising from cleaning, altering or repairing a garment after it has been delivered to you.

Dating and provenance

Where a record says a piece is from the 1980s, that is an estimate drawn from construction details, label design, hardware and fibre content. It is an informed opinion rather than a documented fact, and it is not an authentication or an appraisal.

We do not authenticate designer or branded goods and we do not deal in them. Where a manufacturer's name appears in a record it is there to describe the garment accurately. No affiliation with, sponsorship by or endorsement from any manufacturer is claimed or implied, and all trademarks remain the property of their owners.

Journal articles

Articles in the journal describe how we work and what we have learned. They are not professional textile conservation advice. If you own something of genuine historical value, take it to a conservator rather than following a method written for a shop that sells wearable clothing.

Links to other sites

This site links to a small number of external pages, including browser privacy settings, industry opt-out tools and carrier tracking. We do not control those sites, we are not responsible for their content or their privacy practices, and a link is not an endorsement.

Advertising

This site does not currently display advertising. There are no ad units, no sponsored placements and no promoted listings anywhere on it.

If that changes, any advertising will be clearly labelled with the word Advertisement, kept visually distinct from our own content, and placed below the main content of a page rather than above it. Advertising would be served by third parties, its content would not be selected or endorsed by Vestiary, and an advertisement appearing here would not be a recommendation. Advertising cookies would only ever be set after you accepted the advertising category in our cookie banner, and the details are in the Cookie Policy.

We do not accept advertising that is dressed up to look like our own content, and we do not publish sponsored articles or paid product placements in the journal.

Affiliate disclosure

This website contains no affiliate links. We are not enrolled in any affiliate or referral programme with a third-party retailer, we earn no commission from any outbound link on this site, and no article, product record or journal piece is written to generate commission.

If that ever changes, we will disclose it in two places: here, and immediately next to each affiliate link at the point of use, in plain language, before you click it. That is what the Federal Trade Commission's endorsement guidance requires and it is what we would want as readers.

Our own referral programme, described on the gift cards and referrals page, pays store credit to customers who introduce other customers. It is not an affiliate programme, it pays no cash, and it is open only to people who have ordered from us.

No professional advice

Nothing on this site is legal, financial, medical or professional advice of any kind. The legal pages on this site are templates that describe our own practice and should not be copied for use elsewhere without your own attorney reviewing them.

Availability

We try to keep this site accurate and available, but we do not warrant that it will be uninterrupted or error free. Listings can be wrong, and when they are we correct them and honour the corrected version, as set out in the Terms of Service.
